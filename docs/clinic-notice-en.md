# Notice on the Use of AI in Our Clinic

Our clinic may use AI in a limited way to support the organization of explanations, review wording, and reflect on how treatment plans and communication are presented.

However, AI is **not** used to make final decisions about diagnosis or treatment.  
All responsibility for examination, explanation, judgment, and clinical response remains with the human practitioner.

---

## Situations in which AI may be used

AI may be used in a supportive way for purposes such as:

- reviewing whether explanations are easier to understand
- comparing draft explanations for patients
- organizing how a treatment plan is communicated
- reflecting on ethical aspects of explanation and communication
- reviewing the quality of explanation using anonymized cases

---

## What AI is not used for

In this clinic, AI is **not** used to:

- make diagnoses automatically
- decide urgency or whether medical consultation is needed on its own
- determine treatment plans on its own
- replace direct conversation with patients
- make assumptions about a patient’s faith, values, or worldview

---

## Handling of personal information

Our clinic does **not** enter raw questionnaires or personal information directly into AI systems.

When AI is used, information is handled carefully.  
This may include:

- removing names, addresses, and contact details
- removing workplace, school, or location details that could identify a person
- summarizing or generalizing free-text responses when needed
- handling faith- or worldview-related content carefully so that individuals cannot be identified

---

## Respect for worldview and values

Our clinic considers it important how each patient understands illness and recovery.

For some people, health and suffering are understood not only through scientific explanation, but also through:

- lived experience
- family and relationships
- prayer or faith
- philosophy or life view
- harmony with nature

We try not to dismiss these perspectives, and we also try not to impose them.

If AI is used, we pay attention to this point and place importance on not handling a patient’s meaning, worldview, or faith in a careless or flattening way.

---

## Our basic approach

Our clinic’s basic approach to AI use is as follows:

- AI is used only as a support tool
- judgment remains human
- personal information is protected
- patients’ values and worldviews are respected
- over-reliance on AI is avoided
- transparency is maintained

---

## If you have concerns or preferences

If you have concerns about AI use, or if there are things you do not want included, or ways of explanation you would prefer to avoid, please feel free to let us know.

---

## Additional note

The clinic’s AI policy, anonymization rules, and questionnaire materials are organized and shared as part of the SML-CML project.

Our basic principle is:

**Methods are public. Cases are protected.**
