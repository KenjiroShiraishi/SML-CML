# Worldview and Values Communication Sheet  
## Public Questionnaire for Clinical Use

## Purpose

This questionnaire is intended to support better explanation, dialogue, and trust-building in clinical care.

It is designed to help clinicians understand not only symptoms, but also how a patient interprets illness, recovery, explanation, meaning, and what kinds of responses feel respectful or unhelpful.

This is **not** a diagnostic tool and **not** a personality test.  
There are no right or wrong answers.

Patients may skip any item they do not wish to answer.

This questionnaire alone does not determine treatment policy.

---

## Example situations in which this sheet may be used

This sheet may be used, for example:

- at a first visit
- at a follow-up visit after some trust has developed
- when reviewing treatment direction
- when explanation or dialogue needs adjustment
- when AI-assisted explanation support is being considered  
  (**anonymized summaries only**)

It is not limited to new patients.

---

## Short explanation for patients

This questionnaire is not for diagnosis or personality judgment.  
It is intended to support explanation and dialogue.

You do not need to answer any item you do not wish to answer.

Your responses alone will not determine treatment.

## How to respond

Please choose the option that feels closest to your current view.

- **1** = strongly disagree
- **2** = somewhat disagree
- **3** = neither / unsure
- **4** = somewhat agree
- **5** = strongly agree
