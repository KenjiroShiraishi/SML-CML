# Worldview and Values Communication Sheet  
## Public Questionnaire for Clinical Use

## Purpose

This questionnaire is intended to support better explanation, dialogue, and trust-building in clinical care.

It is designed to help clinicians understand not only symptoms, but also how a patient interprets illness, recovery, explanation, meaning, and what kinds of responses feel respectful or unhelpful.

This is **not** a diagnostic tool and **not** a personality test.  
There are no right or wrong answers.

Patients may skip any item they do not wish to answer.

---

## Suggested use

This sheet may be used:

- at a first visit
- after some trust has been established
- when reviewing treatment direction
- when explanation or communication needs adjustment
- when worldview-sensitive communication seems important
- when AI-assisted explanation support may be considered using anonymized summaries only

It is not limited to new patients.

---

## Response scale

Please choose the option that feels closest to your current view.

- **1** = strongly disagree
- **2** = somewhat disagree
- **3** = neither / unsure
- **4** = somewhat agree
- **5** = strongly agree

## Short note for patients

This questionnaire is used to support explanation and dialogue.  
It is not used by itself to decide treatment.

You do not have to answer every item.

If AI-assisted explanation support is used in this clinic, raw personal forms are not uploaded.  
Only anonymized and generalized summaries may be used when necessary.

---

## Short note for clinical use

This public questionnaire is designed to support worldview-sensitive communication in settings such as:

- acupuncture clinics
- integrative care
- chronic symptom care
- ethically sensitive communication
- small clinical settings where trust depends on more than technical explanation alone

Its purpose is to make room for patients whose understanding of illness may involve not only symptoms and tests, but also lived experience, relationships, values, faith, or broader worldview.

---

## Interpretation note

This sheet should not be used to classify a person into rigid categories.

It is intended as a communication aid.

Possible tendencies that clinicians may notice include:

- stronger preference for objective explanation
- stronger preference for whole-person understanding
- stronger emphasis on lived experience
- stronger need for personal fit rather than general recommendation
- stronger meaning-oriented or worldview-sensitive interpretation

These are starting points for conversation, not final labels.
